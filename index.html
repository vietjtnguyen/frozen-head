Settings and scroll position are written back to the hash as you go, so the URL in the address bar always marks the spot you are looking at.

Drag anywhere to pan; drag the frozen strip up/down to shift which rows it shows; drag the blue bar to resize it; + / - / 0 zoom; browser zoom also works.
